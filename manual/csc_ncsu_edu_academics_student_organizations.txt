All student organizations at NC State, including those in engineering, must register in the new Student Involvement software, CollegiateLink, at
https://getinvolved.ncsu.edu
. That registration should be done by the President or principal officer of the organization.
Any student can create a Profile in CollegiateLink at
https://getinvolved.ncsu.edu/account/profile
and then search and join organizations.
